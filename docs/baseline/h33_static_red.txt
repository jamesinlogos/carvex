468:4. **Limited payload decoding.** By default, JPEG is checked for marker structure, PNG for chunk CRCs, and PDF structurally rather than by rendering. A PNG chunk-CRC mismatch is now an integrity failure: the file is still written with its unchanged score, but `validation=partial`, `validity=suspect` and a `chunk_crc` check name the chunk and offset. Decode mode recomputes every CRC and sets `validated_bytes` to the contiguous prefix ending at the last good chunk. `--validate decode` and B-06 stitches invoke the deep validator. PNG inflation streams IDAT through a fixed buffer and downgrades a declared image above `--max-decode-bytes` (default 256 MiB) to `partial` before inflating; JPEG and PDF decoder internals are not fully instrumented.
